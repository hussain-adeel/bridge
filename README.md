A Modified Version of 'Bridge'
A playing card game

Bridge is played with four players, split into pairs. The team members are usually sat across from each other.

There are x phases in Bridge:

Phase 1: Dealing Cards & Bidding
- Players are delt 5 cards each
- A random player is selected to bid first
    - Bids usually start at 7
- Player selects a suite (the "trump" suite) and the bid amount (number of tricks)
- The next player clockwise (on the opposing team) then has a chance to bid or pass
- This goes on until all playerrs get a turn to bid or pass
- Then players are delt 4 cards each
- Then process continues, the person with the highest bid goes first
- This concludes the bidding
- Players are delt 4 cards each

Phase 2: Making tricks
- The player with the winning bid goes first
- They can place ANY card down
- Then in a clockwise order, each player gets to play a card
    - The starting player decides the suite for that hand
    - If a player has the suite that the starting player played, they must play a card from that suite
    - If they do not have the suite, they may play any other card they have
    - The highest card of that starting suite wins when all 4 players have placed a card,
        - UNLESS a player plays the trump suite (in the case they do not have the played suite)
        - The trump suite is higher than any other suite if played
        - If multiple players play the trump suite, the highest rank will win the hand
- This continues until either:

Phase 3: Game End
- If the player who made the winning bid and their partner make enough tricks to satisfy their bid (in this case the bidding team wins)
- OR it is impossible for the players who made the winning bid to reach their bid amount (in this case the opposing team wins)
